Tasty Swoop / The details

Privacy, in plain words.

Your game stays on your device. Here is what that means, and what happens if you contact us or visit this site.

Last updated 2026-10-30

01

The short version

Tasty Swoop is an offline, single-player puzzle game. You do not need an account. The app does not transmit your gameplay, progress or device-motion data to the developer. This version has no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking or in-app purchases.

02

What stays on your device

The app saves your progress, level stars, best times and scores, saved games, selected shop, language, sound and haptic preferences in its local storage. These records let you continue playing and remember your settings. The developer cannot access them through the app.

03

Device motion and permissions

When you view the map, the app can use device orientation to create a subtle depth effect. This information is processed on your device for that visual effect; it is not sent to the developer or stored as a motion history. The game does not need camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo-library access.

04

Your choices and local data

You can change sound, haptics and language in Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data from that installation. Offloading the app can preserve its data. Apple device backups may also include app data, depending on your settings. There is no developer-hosted account or cloud-save service to recover progress from.

05

If you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. They are used to answer your request and resolve the issue, not for advertising. The email provider also processes the correspondence. Keep sensitive information out of screenshots and messages. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up or legal obligations; you may request its deletion.

06

This website and external services

The support website adds no analytics, advertising trackers, forms or cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and processes hosting data under its privacy statement. Apple may process App Store, device, backup or diagnostic information under its own policies. These services are separate from the app's local gameplay.

07

Children's privacy

The app has no registration, chat or user-generated content and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay. If a child has included personal information in a support message, a parent or guardian can contact the developer to request deletion.

08

Changes and contact

This policy describes the current app. If data practices change, this policy and its date will be updated. For privacy questions or support-message deletion requests, contact Vladyslav Manoilo at vladislavmanoilo@gmail.com.
